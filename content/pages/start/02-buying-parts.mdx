---
title: Do I need to buy stuff?
---

Less than you think. Every build lists its exact parts and total cost up front - most first builds land around $20–30. Read your chosen build's parts list before buying anything, and if it needs an iron, spend the first hour with the [soldering iron guide](/tools/soldering-iron) first. If you get stuck, ask in [the Hack Club Slack](https://hackclub.slack.com).
