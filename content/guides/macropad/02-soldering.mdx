---
title: Soldering
---

<Step title="Solder the diodes">
- Bend each 1N4148 into a staple and seat it in its footprint - the black
  band must match the line printed on the board. Direction matters;
  a diode is a one-way valve for current.
- Solder one leg of each diode first, then check the row: anything tilted
  reheats flat in a second now and never will again later.
- Solder the remaining legs and clip everything flush.
</Step>

<Step title="Seat and solder the switches">
- Press each switch into the plate until it clicks. Both pins must come
  through the PCB - a folded pin looks fine from above and does nothing.
- Solder the two corner switches first and re-check the fit while
  everything is still easy to change.
- Solder the rest. Each joint should look like a small shiny volcano,
  not a ball.
</Step>

<Checkpoint>
Set your <Tool slug="multimeter" /> to continuity mode and probe each
switch's pins while pressing it: every key should beep. No beep means a
cold joint - reheat it. Beeping without a press means a solder bridge - wick it. Faster to find now than after the keycaps go on.
</Checkpoint>
