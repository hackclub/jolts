---
title: How it works
---

Nine keys could eat nine pins, but keyboards are wired as a grid instead:
3 rows × 3 columns = 6 pins. The firmware scans the grid many times a
second, and a diode behind each switch keeps simultaneous presses from
ghosting into keys you never touched. The scan works because each input
pin idles high through a <ConceptLink slug="pull-up-resistors">pull-up resistor</ConceptLink> and gets
pulled low when a key connects it to a driven row.
