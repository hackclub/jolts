A macropad is a little keyboard with keys that do whatever you want:
mute your mic, paste your email, run a build, play a sound. It's the
classic first hardware project because you'll actually use it every day - and because it teaches soldering, firmware, and how keyboards really work
in one weekend.

<PartsList />

<Warning title="Hot iron, real fumes">
Solder in a ventilated space and put the iron in its stand every time it
leaves your hand. If you've never soldered before, spend fifteen minutes
with <Tool slug="soldering-iron" /> first - this guide assumes the basics.
</Warning>
