This part is basically the same as hackpad, so layout, then routing,
except for the mousebites. So in this section I will give some tips for
laying out a split keyboard.

Here is my final pcb:

![Final PCB](https://stasis.hackclub.com/old-cdn/ac8bc9cfffae5f68557d8226a8db07c000685a9e_image.webp)

## If the two halves are symmetrical

<Step title="Mirror the edge cuts">
- Then you should make the edge.cuts for one half, select all the
  edge.cuts, right click, and `flip horizontal`, and now you have two
  symmetrical edge.cuts.
</Step>

## Mousebites layout

<Step title="Place the mousebites">
- You should find two flat sides of your two PCBs, place the mousebites
  there, and edit the edge cuts so it matches the mousebites footprint
  template:

  ![Mousebites on flat side](https://stasis.hackclub.com/old-cdn/23e81cdffe61fe6fedf424bdde600486798ad084_image.webp)
</Step>
