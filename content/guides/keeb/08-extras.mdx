---
title: Adding extra parts
---

Your board can be a lot more than just switches! You can add RGB LEDs,
rotary encoders, sliders, a buzzer, joysticks, and an OLED screen.

A lot of popular keyboards are open source, so you can look at them for
inspiration.

## Finding symbols and footprints

A lot of these can already be found in the default KiCAD libraries or
marbastlib, so you should look in these places first.

If you can't find what you are searching for in the above mentioned
sources, you should search on [SnapEDA](https://www.snapeda.com/) or
[Component Search Engine](https://componentsearchengine.com/).

## Finding 3D models

You should search on [GrabCAD](https://grabcad.com/library) and sort by
most popular, and you will most likely find the model that you are
looking for.
