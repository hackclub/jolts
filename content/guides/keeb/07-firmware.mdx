This page won't cover how you should write your firmware in full detail.
There are a bunch of other resources that cover this topic really well.

We recommend that you use **RMK**, since it's modern and easy to use.
[Here](https://rmk.rs/docs/user_guide/guide_overview.html) is a tutorial
for getting started with RMK.

## The basics

<Step title="Set up your environment">
- Install RMK's CLI and Rust, or use GitHub Actions to automatically
  build your firmware.
</Step>

<Step title="Build your first firmware">
- Configure your keymap and matrix and compile.
</Step>

<Step title="Flash firmware">
- Upload the compiled firmware to your Pico.
</Step>
